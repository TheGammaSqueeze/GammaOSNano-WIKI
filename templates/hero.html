The console interface for GammaOS

GammaOS Nano

GammaOS Nano is the console-style heart of GammaOS, a customized Android firmware for handhelds. It boots you straight into a PlayStation 3 style home with three switchable themes, built-in music, photo and video players, a web browser, IPTV and internet radio, network shares, and fast emulation.
