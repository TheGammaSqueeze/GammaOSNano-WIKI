The console interface for GammaOS

GammaOS Nano

GammaOS Nano is the console-style heart of GammaOS, a customized Android firmware for handhelds. It boots you straight into a PlayStation 3 style home with three switchable themes, built-in music, photo and video players, a web browser, IPTV and internet radio, network shares, and fast emulation. This is the complete guide.

Three themes, one interface

GammaOS Nano ships with three switchable home themes. Pick the one you love in Settings > Theme Settings > Home Theme, and switch any time you feel like a change.

Want the full tour of what each theme offers? Head to Home Themes.

What you can do

  • Launch games from every system through RetroArch cores or standalone emulator apps.
  • Play Nintendo DS games with the built-in DraStic Nano emulator.
  • Enjoy your media: music with visualizers and internet radio, a full-screen photo viewer, and a video player with multi audio track and subtitle support.
  • Browse network shares over SMB, NFS, WebDAV, and FTP, right alongside your local files.
  • Group games into collections, scrape boxart, and sort huge libraries with ease.
  • Tune everything from RGB lighting to speaker EQ in the deep Settings tree and GammaOS Toolbox.

Ready to set up your handheld? Getting Started walks you through it.
