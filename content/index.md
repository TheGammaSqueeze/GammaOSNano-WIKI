## What you can do

- Launch games from every system through RetroArch cores or standalone emulator apps.
- Play Nintendo DS games with the built-in [DraStic Nano](drastic-nano.html) emulator.
- Enjoy your [media](media.html): music with visualizers and internet radio, a full-screen photo viewer, and a video player with multi audio track and subtitle support.
- Browse [network shares](network-shares.html) over SMB, NFS, WebDAV, and FTP, right alongside your local files.
- Group games into [collections](collections.html), scrape [boxart](boxart.html), and sort huge libraries with ease.
- Tune everything from RGB lighting to speaker EQ in the deep [Settings tree](settings-reference.html) and [GammaOS Toolbox](gammaos-toolbox.html).

Ready to set up your handheld? [Getting Started](getting-started.html) walks you through it.
