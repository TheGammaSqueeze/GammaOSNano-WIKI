Keep your games, music, photos, and video on a computer or NAS and let your handheld reach them over the network. Once a share is mounted, it behaves just like storage on the device itself.
{: .lead }

You will find this feature under **Settings > Network Shares**.

## Supported protocols

GammaOS Nano can connect to four kinds of network share:

| Protocol | Common use |
| --- | --- |
| SMB | Windows shared folders and most NAS boxes |
| NFS | Linux and Unix file servers |
| WebDAV | Web-based file storage |
| FTP | Classic file transfer servers |

Make sure Wi-Fi is connected before you try to mount a share. See [Wi-Fi & Bluetooth](network.html) if you need to get online first.
{: .callout .note }

## Where your shares appear

Once a share is connected, it shows up in the **File Explorer** so you can browse and manage its files right on the handheld.

You can also add a share to the media libraries. When you do, the files on it are scanned like local storage, so any music, photos, and video on the share appear in your [Music, Photos & Video](media.html) categories automatically.
